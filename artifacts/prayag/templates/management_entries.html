Management Manual Entries

Grid power, electricity & solar generation, tariff rates, and contractor labour are not in any production workbook — they must be captured here each month (source: JVVL electricity bill, Solar Reading Details, contractor invoices). Values are saved in this app only and never written back to the Google Sheets.

These entries are optional
You can generate any management report without filling these — production, utilisation and efficiency recompute from daily data. Only the power, solar and contractor cost columns wait on these inputs.
