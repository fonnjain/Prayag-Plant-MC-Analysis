How figures are computed

Days of Cover
= Closing Stock ÷ (Avg Consumption Last Month ÷ 30). Recomputed from raw cells — stored "Stock Days" (PIPE only) is a cross-check, not the headline.
Reorder flag
= Cover ≤ Lead time in days. Stock-out likely before replenishment arrives.
Suggested Purchase
= max(Ideal Stock − Closing Stock, Min Batch) when reorder flag is set.
Ideal Stock (BOP/Report-3, PIPE)
Derived from Buffer Stock (days) × daily consumption when no unit qty is given.
