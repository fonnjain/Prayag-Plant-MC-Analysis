R-30 — Two relationship checks (per spec)
(1) Weight Loss = Batch Weight − Material out of Mixer — must hold by construction.
(2) Av. Weight Loss % = Weight Loss ÷ Material out of Mixer (spec denominator). Our implementation uses batch weight as the denominator (loss / batch). The difference is small (≤0.06 pp) but the UPVC row is the distinguishing test: sheet shows 0.51% (÷ material), our figure is 0.50% (÷ batch). Both denominators are reported below.
