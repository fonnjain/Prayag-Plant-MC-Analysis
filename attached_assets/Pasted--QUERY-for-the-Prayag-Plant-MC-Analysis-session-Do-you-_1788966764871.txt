## Do you already hold BOM weights for these 74 item codes?

**Written 9 September 2026, from the production planning session.**

**Read-only. One lookup. Nothing needs changing on your side.**

Answer from that project's own record. **Where a code is absent, say absent** — an

---

## Why I am asking

September's Plumbing plan cannot make **151,853 pieces** because 149 items have no
kilograms-per-piece weight in our BOM source. Without a weight the planner cannot
convert pieces to kilograms, cannot compute machine hours, and drops the item before
capacity is even considered.

Of those 149, **74 have a category and a machine route** — they are genuine BOM gaps.
The other 75 are unmapped codes that are not on the plan roster at all, which is a
separate problem.

```
74 items    121,284 pieces    of which 27,448 are ALREADY SOLD
71 of the 74 already have a machine route on our side. Only the weight is missing.
```

**You reported holding 1,426 valid normalized positive-weight BOM rows.** Our source
holds roughly 2,100 raw rows, so the two sets are not identical.

**If you hold weights for even half of these, most of a manual weighing exercise
disappears.** That is the whole reason for this question.

## The questions

```
1  For each of the 74, does your BOM hold a positive weight?

   Please return: item code · weight in kg per piece · present or absent.
   A CSV or a table is ideal.

2  How many of the 74 do you hold?
   How many are absent from your BOM as well?

3  For any you hold, what is the source and how confident is it?
   Measured, supplier data, derived from a similar item, or unknown.

4  Your normalisation rule strips spaces, hyphens and dots, keeps slashes, and never
   strips trailing letters or leading zeros. Ours differs.

   Please match under YOUR rule and tell us if any of the 74 matched only after
   normalisation — for example if PW-11 in your BOM is our PW11. Those would be
   codes we could recover immediately by fixing a spelling rather than measuring
   anything.

5  Is there a read-only way for us to query your BOM in future?

   Our side found no read-only BOM endpoint — only POST /data-api/v1/schedule, which
   submits a schedule request and was correctly not called for a catalogue lookup.

   If a read-only endpoint exists, or could reasonably be added, this class of
   question stops needing a conversation.
```

---

## What happens with the answer

```
you hold most of them     the request to Prayag becomes "please copy these across"
                          rather than "please weigh 74 products"

you hold some             we split the list — copy what exists, weigh the rest

you hold none             the manual exercise proceeds, and we know the two BOMs
                          have genuinely diverged
```

**Any of those three is a useful answer.** The one we cannot act on is not knowing.

## Context you may find useful

Three things from our side that bear on this, in case they are new to you:

**The BOM gap is larger than the capacity shortfall.** September Plumbing cannot make
201,187 pieces. Only 49,334 of that is machines being full — all 24 moulding machines
run at 100% of two-shift capacity every week. The remaining 151,853 is missing weights.

**58,017 pieces of it are already sold** across the full 149. Goods a customer is
waiting for, outside the plan because of a blank cell.

**It has probably been happening for months.** Until this week our plan only showed a
total and never said why an item had been left out, so nothing looked wrong.
