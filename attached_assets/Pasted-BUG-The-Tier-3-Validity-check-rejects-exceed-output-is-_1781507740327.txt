BUG: The Tier 3 "Validity" check (rejects-exceed-output) is producing false 
positives and wrongly excluding good rows from the published figures.

WHAT'S HAPPENING
The validity tier flags 27 rows as "Rejects (X) exceed output (Y)" for May 2026 
(26 PTMT machines on tab Report-5, plus Pipe M/C-5 on tab Report-11) and marks 
them HELD ASIDE, which drops them from the published numbers and triggers the 
"Must fix 27 reject-exceeds-output errors before sign-off" readiness verdict.

I verified every flagged row against the source workbooks. In ALL 27 cases, 
output massively exceeds rejects. These are not real errors. The reject value 
the check reports is correct, but the "output" value it compares against is the 
WRONG cell — it is far too small to be the machine's output.

EVIDENCE (source = "PTMT Date Sheet & Monthly Report - May '2026", tab Report-5)
  - 80-4:   flag "Rejects 40 > output 38".  Source: rejection 39.7 kg, output 651.8 kg
  - 250-2:  flag "Rejects 393 > output 118". Source: rejection 392.7 kg, output 3,189 kg
  - 150-4:  flag "Rejects 238 > output 12".  Source: rejection 238.3 kg, output 1,673 kg
  - N-110C: flag "Rejects 242 > output 5".   Source: rejection 242.1 kg, output 1,410 kg
  Pipe M/C-5 (tab Report-11), May total: output 11,328 kg vs rejects 1,180 kg.

The reported "Rejects (X)" exactly matches the source column 
"Actual Rejection Weight (in Kgs)" (rounded). The reported "output (Y)" matches 
NO output column in the sheet — real monthly net output ranges ~650–5,000 kg 
per machine.

ROOT CAUSE TO FIND AND FIX
In the validity-tier code, locate where "output" is read for the 
reject-vs-output comparison. It is pulling the wrong column (or a different unit, 
or a per-hour/ideal cell). It must read the same net output the rest of the 
dashboard uses:
  - PTMT Report-5: output = "Wt in Kgs" column (net monthly output, kg). 
    Reject = "Actual Rejection Weight (in Kgs)". Both must be kg.
  - Pipe Report-11 / its summary: output and rejection must both be compared in 
    KG (do not compare reject-kg against output-pieces, and do not use 
    ideal-output-per-hour as "output").

Add a guard: only flag a row as a validity error when both values are present, 
in the same unit (kg), and reject > output. If the output cell is blank/zero or 
in a different unit, do not flag.

ALSO FIX THE DOWNSTREAM EFFECTS
  - Stop holding these rows aside; they are valid and must be included in the 
    published PTMT and Pipe figures for May 2026 (currently understated).
  - Recompute the "held aside" count and the readiness verdict after the fix.
