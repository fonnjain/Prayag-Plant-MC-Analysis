# Garden — Capture Rejection-Only Machine-Dates (155 kg silently dropped)

Read `PRAYAG_RULES.md` first. Touches **R-06, R-08, R-35**.

Repo: `fonnjain/Prayag-Plant-MC-Analysis`.

---

## The defect (root cause confirmed by live diagnostic)

The Daily Report rejection join landed correctly — APR 3.06% and JUN 3.31% match source exactly. But **July shows 5.28% against a true 5.76%**, with **155 kg unattributed**:

| M/C | Date | Rej kg | DR output | Block-tab record? | Transferred |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| M/C-2 | 2026-07-31 | 70.00 | 0.00 | **No** | **dropped** |
| M/C-3 | 2026-07-10 | 1,513.50 | 0.00 | Yes | ✓ |
| M/C-3 | 2026-07-27 | 95.00 | 1,378.30 | Yes | ✓ |
| M/C-3 | 2026-07-30 | 90.00 | 1,352.90 | Yes | ✓ |
| M/C-3 | 2026-07-31 | 85.00 | 0.00 | **No** | **dropped** |

**The maps are not the problem.** `rej_map` and `dr_out_map` are written in the same unconditional loop, so their key sets are identical and both dropped rows are present in both. The limiting factor is the **outer transfer loop, which iterates over block-tab records** — a rejection whose `(machine, date)` has no block-tab row has no `r` to attach to and is discarded with no warning.

Both dropped rows are **31 July with zero output** — a month-end rejection entry booked against no production. That is a legitimate and probably recurring business pattern, so this will drop silently every month it occurs. It fails in the worst way: no error, just a plausible-looking figure that is too low (**R-08**, **R-35**).

---

## Required change

**Emit a synthetic record for rejection-only machine-dates**, so rejection with no matching production row is still counted.

1. After the existing transfer loop, identify keys present in `rej_map` with a non-zero rejection that were **not** matched to any block-tab record.
2. For each, append a Record carrying: the machine label (same `machine_prefix` convention as the block rows), the date, `total_count = 0`, `reject_count` from `rej_map`, `reject_denominator` from `dr_out_map` (may be 0), and `rejection_tracked = True`.
3. These records must roll up into the segment rejection total exactly as normal records do. They must **not** add output, hours, or utilisation.
4. Do **not** change how matched rows are handled. Do **not** change output figures.

### Also required — surface it (R-35)

These records represent rejection booked against no production. Note them in the existing warning / data-health mechanism, e.g.:

> `GARDEN 2026-07: 155 kg of rejection recorded on machine-dates with no production row (M/C-2 31-Jul 70 kg, M/C-3 31-Jul 85 kg) — counted in the rejection total, shown separately as no output is attributed to those dates.`

The point is that a reader can see the figure is complete *and* understand why those dates carry rejection without output.
