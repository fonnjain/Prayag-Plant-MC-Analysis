| Paid Hours Devoted by Per Person | **1,121** | 301 + 273 + 297 + 251 | **278.9** (59,967 ÷ 215) |

**Compute ours correctly and flag each divergence**, following the pattern already used for `Avg. Per Output on Hours`.

## And `Per KG Labour Cost` has a period mismatch

The sheet's TOTAL reads **3.53**. But July wages are blank while July output is counted, so the numerator covers three months and the denominator four:

```
20,09,948 (Apr–Jun wages)  ÷  537,109 (Apr–Jul output)  =  3.74     ← not 3.53 either
20,09,948 (Apr–Jun wages)  ÷  364,469 (Apr–Jun output)  =  5.51     ← like-for-like
```

Neither reproduces 3.53, so its derivation is unclear. **Report what the sheet shows, compute ours on a like-for-like basis, and flag the mismatch.** Better still: render the TOTAL as **AWAITING** until July wages arrive, consistent with every other report in the series.
