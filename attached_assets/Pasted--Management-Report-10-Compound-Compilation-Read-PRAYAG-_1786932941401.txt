## Two relationships to verify, not assume

Both hold across all six populated columns in the source. **Confirm they hold in our data before wiring, and report if they do not** (**R-30**):

```
Total Weight Loss at Mixer  =  Total Batch Weight  −  Total Material out of Mixer
  CPVC   359,296.90 − 356,464.00 = 2,832.90   ✓
  UPVC   536,135.40 − 533,430.00 = 2,705.40   ✓
  SWR    723,501.20 − 719,913.00 = 3,588.20   ✓

Av. Weight Loss %age  =  Weight Loss  ÷  Total Material out of Mixer
  UPVC   2,705.40 ÷ 533,430.00 = 0.51%   (÷ batch weight gives 0.50% — the sheet shows 0.51%)
  TOTAL  12,015.44 ÷ 2,163,076.00 = 0.56%
```

The UPVC row is what distinguishes the two possible denominators, so use it as the test. **State which denominator our implementation uses and why.**
