## Part 2 — New violations introduced by the fixes

The remediation touched source paths, label handling, period grain and FY windowing across four builders. Check what that may have disturbed.

**Label normalizer.** Pipe's normalizer turned any trailing digit into `M/C-n`, so `PIPE Grinder-1` became `M/C-1`. It now requires an explicit `M/C` token, plus an `is_finishing` guard. Confirm:
- No finishing record can reach a primary-machine rollup in **any** report.
- Moulding and GOM use a stricter helper — confirm they also apply the `is_finishing` guard, not just safe label matching. Grinder and Pulverizer rows carry zero this window, so safety cannot be inferred from the totals.

**Period grain.** Report-11-only records stored `period=<full date>` instead of the month, which caused a valid 858 kg June row to be dropped. Confirm the grain is now monthly everywhere, and that no other loader has the same defect.

**FY windowing.** A cutoff month was added to Pipe, Moulding and GOM. Confirm it excludes August without excluding anything within April–July, and that page and export pass the same month.

**The net/gross contract.** `accum_record_kg()` is now plant-aware: Garden, Garden WB and HDPE treat `total_count` as net; PTMT subtracts rejection. Confirm the same rule is applied consistently by Part B and by each report's own builder — and report **any place that still applies one blanket rule to all plants.**
