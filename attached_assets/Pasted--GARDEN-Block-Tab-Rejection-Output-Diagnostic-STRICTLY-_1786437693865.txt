## CORRECTED CONTEXT — read this before starting

A previous report stated GARDEN's plant view is fed by "monthly-live records from the annual `mc_grid` tab." **That is incorrect.** Per `app.py::get_data`:

> *"The daily files are the SOURCE OF TRUTH for every period. Monthly and FY headline totals are summed from the authoritative daily tabs, not the monthly summary grid… Daily totals are never 'reconciled down' to the lower summary figures."*
> *"Months in this period without a daily workbook show no data — the monthly summary is not substituted."*

So for any month with a daily file, the plant view reads **daily records only** — there is no mixing of daily and monthly grains, and no double-counting. For GARDEN that means the source is the per-machine **`MACHINE n` block tabs** via `sheets._emit_blocks` → `parsers.parse_daily_blocks`, with run hours joined separately from the `Daily Report` matrix (`runhours_tab`).

`parse_daily_blocks` **does** read rejection — its docstring states output and *"rejection (any `REJECT*` column)"* are both detected from the header band, never by fixed index. `_emit_blocks` also collects **`block_notes`** specifically for *"rejection-header-present-but-unmatched drift per tab"* (deduped across machine tabs).

**Therefore the likeliest cause of 0.0% is that drift condition, not a routing gap.** This diagnostic is to confirm or refute that.
