CONTEXT — Pipe & Fitting daily workbooks: the FY 2025-26 files use a DIFFERENT column
layout than FY 2026-27. Our parsers currently assume fixed column positions and therefore
read wrong columns on the older files. Fix parsing to locate columns by HEADER TEXT, never
by fixed index, and apply the correct reconciliation. Do not change FY 2026-27 behaviour.

SCOPE: All 12 FY 2025-26 Pipe & Fitting "… Plant Date Sheet & Monthly Report" workbooks
(Apr-2025 … Mar-2026).

1) REPORT-11 (pipe daily) — header is on row 5. Resolve these by header text:
   - Machine = "MACHINE NO." (values like "PIPE M/C - 1"); normalize to the integer N.
   - Production weight = the standalone column titled exactly "Weight" (the one immediately
     after "Pcs"). DO NOT use "Ideal Weight (KG)".
   - Rejection = "Actual Wt (Kg)". "FC" is a separate column — keep it separate, not in rejection.
   - Pipe type = the FIRST "TYPES" column (CPVC/UPVC/SWR/AGRI). There is a DUPLICATE "TYPES"
     column further right (~col 22) — ignore it.

2) REPORT-12 (moulding daily) — in FY 2025-26 this is shifted one column LEFT vs FY 2026-27.
   Resolve by header text:
   - Find the "Moulding Machine" header → column M (it is col 4 in FY25-26, col 5 in FY26-27).
   - Output kg = the "Wt in Kgs" sub-column under "Output Production" = column M+5
     (col 9 in FY25-26, col 10 in FY26-27).
   - Rejection = the column whose header contains "Actual Rejection" (col 25 in FY25-26,
     col 26 in FY26-27).

3) SOURCING RULE:
   - Pipe production & rejection: take from REPORT-11 (daily), validated against Report-13
     (Report-13 = daily by-type pivot of Report-11; they must tie to the kg). Do NOT use
     Report-5 for pipe — it is unreliable (it matched in most FY25-26 months but broke in
     Mar-2026, reading only 49% of the daily log).
   - Moulding production & rejection: REPORT-12 (daily) is authoritative; Report-5 moulding
     agrees and may be used only as a cross-check.
