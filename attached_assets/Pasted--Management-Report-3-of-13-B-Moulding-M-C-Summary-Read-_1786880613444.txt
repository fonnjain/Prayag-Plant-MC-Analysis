## Three things to get right

**`Avg. Per Output on Hours` is a sum of the band averages, not a weighted mean.** TOTAL reads **76.10**, and 5.38 + 9.18 + 11.91 + 12.63 + 15.22 + 21.79 = 76.11. Reproduce the sheet's convention to match the layout, and **add a code comment saying so**, or someone will later "correct" it into a weighted average and break the match.
