  1. FORMAT comes from the final sheet: read its header/label block to learn the exact
     column order, column labels, units (kg / % / Ltr / INR), row/machine order, grouping,
     and number formatting. Reproduce that structure verbatim in the app output.

     ANY sheet (daily or final) are never trusted — recompute every ratio from raw cells

  3. After computing, RECONCILE each report to its final sheet (value-by-value where aligned):
     show match % and flag any cell differing by >3%. Where they differ, the recomputed
