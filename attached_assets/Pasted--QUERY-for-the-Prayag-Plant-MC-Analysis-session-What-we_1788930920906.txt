**This is the part we most need. Everything else is secondary.**

```

     per day, in pieces and/or kilograms? Report whatever unit you hold it in.

A2   How is that capacity derived — observed maximum, p90 of observed output,
     rated machine speed, standard cycle time, or something else? Name the
     method for each figure.

A4   Which machines or pools are shared between product groups? The planning
     side has been told that where two categories run on the same machines they

     machines twice. Which Plumbing groups share?

A5   MC7 and MC8 are recorded on our side as locked out. What does that mean —
     under maintenance, decommissioned, reserved for another segment? Are they

A6   Is capacity held per machine, per pool, per product group, or per item? If
     per item, we would need the mapping from item to machine.

A7   What is the total Plumbing plant capacity per day, and per month at the
     September working-day count?
```

**If A1 can be answered as a table — pool, capacity, unit, method — that alone
would let us fit the plan.**
