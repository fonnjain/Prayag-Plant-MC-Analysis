- **R-43** on the Fittings column of both Ideal Cost sheets — the source's Fittings figure is a **piece count** (APR 13,40,117) multiplied by a per-kg rate, inflating its ideal cost ~15×; ours uses kilograms and is the correct basis.

- **Basis labels** — production is **gross** in costing (rejection included), matching the workbook's own note; the production pages use net. Label it per **R-22**.
- **`AWAITING SOURCE DATA`** for July wages must render as that text, never 0 or blank (**R-07**, **R-08**).
