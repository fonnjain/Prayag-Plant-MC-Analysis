## FIX 1 — Kilogram output is captured but never shown

The QA review asked for Tank in kilograms as well as litres, and pointed at the exact columns — **KH M · VN N · WB L**. The data is already on every Tank record in `secondary_counts["kg"]`:

| Plant | Apr | May | Jun | Jul |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| VN | 5,395.80 | 13,535.00 | 13,526.00 | 14,548.90 |
| WB | 11,163.00 | — | — | — |
| KH | — | 21,090.00 | 30,490.50 | — |

Render kilograms alongside litres on the Tank pages, with **both units labelled**. Litres remains the headline; kilograms is the figure that makes Tank comparable with every other segment and costable per kilogram.

Do not convert between them — the kilogram figure is a tank's own weight, not a conversion from its capacity. They are independent measures of the same production (**R-09**).

## FIX 2 — Rejection: two bases, only one shown

Tank records carry two valid rejection measures, and the page currently shows only the first:

| Basis | Source | VN July | Rate |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| **Litres** | `reject_count` (rejected pcs × tank size) | 5,500 Ltr | **0.97%** of 565,500 Ltr |
| **Kilograms** | `rej_base_kg` + `rej_mouth_kg` | 449.30 kg | **3.09%** of 14,548.90 kg |

**The plant's own dashboard reports 3.09%**, and both source worksheets agree on it (`PROD. REPORT` 449.30, `DAILY REPORT` 446.40). An unlabelled **0.97%** on our page will read as an error to anyone comparing against the plant's figure.

Show both, each with its basis named (**R-22**). Neither is *the* rejection figure: litres measures capacity scrapped, kilograms measures material scrapped including mouth lids. They answer different questions.

Where a plant records only one basis, show that one and leave the other blank — never a zero (**R-08**).

## Next pass, not this one — WB April per-machine

The data supports fixing it properly rather than relabelling: **MACHINE-2's row carries the full per-date grid**, identical to the TOTAL row, so attribution is provable rather than inferred. Parsing that row directly would give a real per-machine view with MACHINE-1 correctly showing 0.

That is a parser change and belongs in its own pass. Also carry forward the WB April internal discrepancy — TOTAL row **98 h** against a per-date sum of **100 h**; the per-date sum is used and the 2-hour gap stays flagged (**R-35**).
