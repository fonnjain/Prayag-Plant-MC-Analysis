=== FIX 1 — label the production basis (the only real gap) ===
The costing module reports on a GROSS basis (production includes rejection — the workbook
states "Production in KG has Rejection included"). The main production pages report NET for
Garden, Moulding and PTMT, and GROSS for PIPE. Nothing on any page says which.

Consequence: the workbook shows Garden rejection at 2.97% / 3.20%; the app shows 3.06% /
3.31%. Both are correct — the denominators differ. Nothing explains this, so a reader
comparing them concludes one is wrong.

Add a short basis label wherever a production figure or rejection % is shown — e.g.
"gross (rejection included)" or "net". Use the existing info()/tooltip mechanism; do not
invent a new one. On the costing page state plainly that its figures are gross.

DO NOT change any denominator, any calculation, or which basis any page uses. Label only.
