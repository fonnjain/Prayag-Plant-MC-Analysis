## Block EF.1 — MD.1 must use the effective multiplier

```markdown
--- THE DEFECT ---

MD.1 reads `factorsJson` as the app multiplier. **factorsJson is not the effective
multiplier** — it records an older base category value, while the frozen rows were
computed with the month-scoped suggested multiplier.

So the decomposition attributes a real multiplier difference to residual, which is
exactly what happened on three reports.

--- WHAT ---

  a) Derive the effective multiplier per category from the FROZEN ROWS —
     buffer requirement ÷ three-month average — rather than reading factorsJson.
  b) Where they differ, show BOTH: "effective 1.14 · recorded 1.5" with the
     discrepancy flagged.
  c) The multiplier effect is then computed at the effective value, and the residual
     becomes the genuine input difference.

--- WHY BOTH, NOT JUST THE EFFECTIVE ONE ---

A run whose recorded factor does not match the rows it produced is itself a finding.
Silently using the effective value would hide it.

  d) Add a check: does the recorded factor match the effective one? Report PASS or
     the discrepancy per category.

--- WHAT IT SHOULD PRODUCE ---

On #2697, after the fix:

  Accessorise    residual near −697, not −9,757
  Faucets        residual near −166, not −14,796
  Cistern        residual near −1, not +15,048
  Cocks Standard residual near +20,703, unchanged — that one is real

**REPORT 1's residual should NOT move.** It is a genuine pending-input difference and
the fix must not absorb it.
```
