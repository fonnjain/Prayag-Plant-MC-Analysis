Read PRAYAG_RULES.md. Two changes from the throttling diagnostic. Both are about how transient
failures are handled, not about any source or figure.

No figure may change. No Sheets writes (R-21). No cache clearing (R-34).

=== 1. Carry the per-pair failure reason through to the page ===
The loader already captures it — failed_reasons at sheets.py:2896 — but the builder discards it
and emits a generic "could not be read". That is why the May failure could not be classified
after the fact and needed a full investigation to rule out a broken workbook.

Surface the reason alongside the failed month. The four cases need to be distinguishable:
  429 / rate limited      -> transient, will clear
  timeout                 -> transient, will clear
  permission / 404        -> the file or its sharing changed
  completeness classifier -> population below its high-water mark; the workbook may have changed

Keep it short in the cell or warning; the detail can sit in a tooltip. Do NOT print file IDs in
visible text — that was fixed in the presentation round.

Apply wherever _failed_pairs is already consumed: the eight builders plus app.py's get_data
path. Do not widen it further.

=== 2. Pace the Pipe Summary's external fan-out ===
One page load fires: several daily PIPE workbooks, the DASHBOARD tab, the FY25-26 comparison
tab, and four separate payroll workbooks. That fan-out caused the ~51s build and the 429 burst
that hit several reads at once.

Add bounded retry with backoff for transient 429 and timeout only. Constraints:
 - a bounded number of attempts, then give up and report partial — never retry indefinitely
 - do NOT retry permission, 404, or completeness failures; those will not clear
 - a partial result must still be uncached and visibly partial (R-06, R-07, Failure Mode #9).
   That behaviour is correct today and must not change.
 - report the retry policy you choose and why

Before implementing, report whether the reads can be paced or batched rather than only retried —
fewer calls would be better than more attempts at the same number.
