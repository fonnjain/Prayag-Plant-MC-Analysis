## Q1 — Gross or net? (R-22)

The workbook states explicitly, on four tabs: **"Note: Production in KG has Rejection included."** Verified exactly for Garden — 40,141 gross − 1,191 rejection = 38,950 net, matching the Daily Report; and 69,126 − 2,215 = 66,911.

This also explains a difference we had noted: the workbook's Garden rejection percentages (APR 2.97%, JUN 3.20%) are computed on **gross**, ours (3.06%, 3.31%) on **net**. Neither is wrong.

Report, for each segment:
- which basis the app's **production** figure uses — gross or net;
- which basis the app's **rejection %** denominator uses;
- whether any page shows a gross figure and a net-based percentage together, or labels either explicitly.

**Do not change anything.** The question is whether a reader can tell which basis they are looking at.
