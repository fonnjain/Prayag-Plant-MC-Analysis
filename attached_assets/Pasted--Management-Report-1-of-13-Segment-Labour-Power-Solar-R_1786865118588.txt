## THE RULE THAT GOVERNS ALL THIRTEEN REPORTS

> **Layout is not data.** These reports must match their Excel counterparts exactly — same columns, same order, same headers, same TOTAL row, same month ordering. **But every number comes from the app's own recomputation.** Where our figure differs from the sheet's, **show ours and flag the difference**. Never copy a cell across to make them agree.

The Google Sheets are **verification material only** while Prayag runs both in parallel. A mismatch means *investigate*, never *adjust*.

- **Labour and wages** — Segment Cost workbook, dedicated per-segment tabs (**R-11**). Never from a UNIT roll-up row: UNIT-3's `TOTAL` column is **headcount**, not wages.
- **Production (Kgs)** — each segment's own recomputed output. Note the costing module works on a **gross** basis (rejection included), matching the workbook's own note; production pages use net. **Label which basis the column shows** (**R-22**).
- **Power and solar** — `costing_power.py` already reads JVVL amount, electricity kWh, solar generation and both tariff scenarios (7.08 and 11.5). The sheet's layout shows only the 7.08 column; keep 11.5 available but match the sheet's visible columns.

**Tank production is in litres**, and the column asks for kilograms. Tank kg exists in `secondary_counts["kg"]` — use it, and never convert litres to kilograms (**R-09**).
