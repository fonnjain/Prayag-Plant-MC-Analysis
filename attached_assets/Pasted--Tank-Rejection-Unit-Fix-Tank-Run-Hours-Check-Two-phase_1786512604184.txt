## PHASE 1 — Tank rejection is being read in KILOGRAMS and displayed as LITRES

**Verified from source** (TANK VN, June 2026, `1Vsba-WDcYwSstEZsX37ntm_N05yPn0T5DzSkls9zRUw`). The daily `PROD. REPORT` / DAILY PRODUCTION table has **three separate rejection columns**:

| Column header (verbatim) | VN June TOTAL |
|---|---:|
| `REJECTION IN PCS.` | 9 |
| `REJECTION MOUTH LID IN KG.` | 291.3 |
| `REJECTION IN KG.` | 221.2 |

The app currently reports **513** for VN June. That is `291.3 + 221.2 = 512.5` — **two unrelated kilogram columns added together**, then rendered under a Litres heading. It is not a meaningful quantity in any unit.

**The correct litre figure** is `REJECTION IN PCS.` × `SIZE (LTR.)`, summed per row. For VN June the nine rejected tanks were 2×1000 + 1×1000 + 1×1000 + 1×500 + 2×1000 + 2×1000 = **8,500 Ltr**, which matches the annual VN June rejection of **8,500 Ltr exactly**.

Production already reconciles perfectly (daily 533,500 Ltr / 735 pcs vs annual 533,500 Ltr / 735 pcs), so **only rejection is wrong**.

### Do

1. In the tank parser, locate columns **by header text** (slash- and case-tolerant): `SIZE (LTR.)`, `PRODUCTION IN PCS.`, `PRODUCTION IN LTR.`, `REJECTION IN PCS.`, `REJECTION IN KG.`, `REJECTION MOUTH LID IN KG.`
2. Compute **rejection in litres = Σ (REJECTION IN PCS. × SIZE (LTR.))** per row. This is the figure the Tank reports display.
3. **Stop summing the two kg columns.** Keep them available separately if wanted, but never add mouth-lid kg to tank kg, and never label a kg figure as Ltr.
4. Assert the pcs and size columns are found; **raise a named error** if not — do not silently fall back to a kg column.

### Validate (report observed vs target)

- **VN June: 8,500 Ltr** rejection (against annual 8,500) and 533,500 Ltr / 735 pcs production.
- Recompute VN, WB and KH for **Apr–Jul** and report the new litre rejection per plant per month. Expect totals far above the current ~6,139 Ltr and roughly in line with the annual's 86,500 Ltr — **report the observed figures; do not adjust anything to hit that number.**
- Rejection % must be `rejection Ltr ÷ production Ltr`. No kg anywhere in the Tank rejection path.
- Confirm no labour/hours are fabricated for Tank and that production figures are unchanged.

**STOP. Report before Phase 2.**
