## FIX 1 — Tank utilisation is fabricated (the live defect)

Closing R-25 emptied `PLANTS_WITHOUT_RUNHOURS`, so `runhours_tracked` is now true for Tank and `ideal_hours.resolve()` runs. Its precedence is override → sheet value → app default, and `APP_DEFAULT_IDEAL_HOURS` still contains:

```python
"TANK": 500.0,     # Tanks (KH) — app-logic default (not in sheet)
```

Tank has **no ideal hours in any sheet** and **no `baselines.json` entry**, so every Tank plant-month now falls through to that 500-hour default. Two consequences, both wrong:

- **VN July renders 232 ÷ 500 = 46.4% utilisation** against a figure nobody supplied.
- **KH (all months) and WB (May–July) render 0 ÷ 500 = 0.0%** — a **green zero** that reads as "the plant was idle" rather than "this is not recorded." That is Failure Mode #14 in a third place, after Garden and HDPE.

`baselines.json` states the position plainly: *ship only real, business-supplied planned hours; do NOT enter estimates or the sheet's flat 500-h placeholder.* That is **R-16**, and a percentage built on that placeholder is fabricated (**R-07**).

### Required change

**Remove `"TANK"` from `APP_DEFAULT_IDEAL_HOURS`.** With no override, no sheet value and no default, `resolve()` returns `(None, SRC_NOT_SET)`, `util_available` stays false, and utilisation is suppressed — exactly as `_metrics_json` intends.

Leave `GARDEN` and `HDPE` defaults alone; they are a separate question.

Add a comment recording why, so it is not reinstated: Tank has real run hours (R-39) but **no ideal hours from the business**, so utilisation stays suppressed until planned hours are supplied. Having hours is not the same as having a standard to measure them against.

### Validate

- **Reconciled hours still display**: VN Apr **76** · May **196** · Jun **208** · Jul **232**; WB Apr **100**.
- **Utilisation is blank / n/a on every Tank plant-month** — not 46.4%, not 0.0%.
- Print, per Tank plant-month: `actual_hours`, `ideal_hours`, the **ideal-hours source** (must read `not_set`), and the displayed utilisation.
- **No other segment's utilisation moves**: Garden Apr 27.5% · Jun 66.9%; HDPE May ≈3.8%; PIPE, MOULDING, PTMT unchanged. If any moves, **stop** — the default was shared.
- Tank production unchanged: VN Jun **533,500 Ltr** · WB Jul **1,702,000** · KH Jun **1,419,500**; VN Jun still reconciles to its annual at **±0**.


This may be the correct interim: `PROD. REPORT` has no machine column, so per-machine attribution of output genuinely is not available, and splitting it would be invention (**R-07**).

**Do not change the behaviour in this pass.** Instead:
