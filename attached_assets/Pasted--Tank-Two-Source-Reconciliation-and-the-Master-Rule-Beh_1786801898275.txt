> ## R-39 · Where a segment records the same production twice, neither source is authoritative
>
> Several segments record the same month in **two worksheets at different grains**: one **per machine × date** (carrying run hours), one **per item** (carrying product detail). They are not duplicates and they are not alternatives. **Each is systematically incomplete in a way the other is not.**
>
> **The rule: reconcile by taking the date-wise maximum over the union of every (machine, date) cell either source reports. Never choose one source. Never add them together.**
>
> **Why neither is complete:**
> - The **item-level** sheet has no row for a day that produced nothing, because there is nothing to itemise — so it silently loses machine-days that ran with zero output.
> - The **machine-level** sheet is a hand-maintained summary — so it silently loses days when nobody filled it in.
>
> **Proven twice, in opposite directions:**
> - **PIPE** — Report-5 (per machine-date; run hours; no pipe type) against Report-11 (item journal; type; no hours). April 2026: six machine-days appear **only** in Report-11. Date-wise max reproduced the audited 157,883 kg.
> - **TANK** — `DAILY REPORT` (per machine-date; run hours, output kg, rejection kg) against `PROD. REPORT` (per item; date, code, size, colour, pcs, Ltr, kg). VN July 2026: **19 July appears only in `DAILY REPORT`** — 8 hours run, zero output, so `PROD. REPORT` has no row. 232 hrs against 224 hrs, a difference of exactly that one day.
>
> **A difference between the two totals is not an error.** It is the measure of what one source is missing, and **its sign can reverse month to month** — VN June −24 hrs, VN July +8 hrs. A sign flip is diagnostic of two incomplete sources, not of a contradiction to be resolved by picking a favourite.
>
> **What is a genuine error:** the two sources disagreeing on a quantity they both fully record. VN July output agrees to **0.014%** (14,548.9 kg against 14,546.8 kg). A material divergence there means something is wrong and must be surfaced (**R-35**).
>
> **When a join produces zero matches, warn** (**R-27**) — a silent fallback to a single source disables the reconciliation without telling anyone.

Also add, as a correction to the record:

> **Failure Mode #15 — assuming a two-source difference means one source is wrong.** Tank's 208-vs-184 and 224-vs-232 hour discrepancies were treated for weeks as a contradiction blocking all Tank utilisation. They were never a contradiction: each sheet was missing days the other held. The same shape had already been solved for PIPE, and the solution was not carried across.

## PHASE 2 — Implement (only after Phase 1 is reviewed)

Follow `pipe_reconcile.py`'s structure. **Do not modify `pipe_reconcile.py` itself (R-15).**

**Hours** — date-wise max over the union of dates from both tabs, per machine. Where only one source has a date, that source's value stands. Where `DAILY REPORT` is empty for a plant-month, hours remain unavailable and utilisation stays suppressed (**R-07**).

**Machine dimension** — take it from `DAILY REPORT`, which is the only tab that carries one. Tank records currently have `machine=""`, which is why the machine view is empty. Where `DAILY REPORT` is unfilled the machine view must state that machine-level data is not recorded for that plant, rather than rendering blank (**R-35**).

**Output** — keep `PROD. REPORT` as the source. It carries item, size and colour detail that `DAILY REPORT` lacks, and the two agree on kg. Report the cross-check delta per month; do not reconcile it away.

**Rejection — surface both bases, labelled.** Tank has two valid and different measures:

| Basis | How it is derived | VN July | Rate |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| **Litres** | rejected pcs × tank size | 5,500 Ltr | **0.97%** of 565,500 Ltr |
| **Kilograms** | `REJECTION IN KG` + `REJECTION MOUTH LID IN KG` | 449.3 kg | **3.09%** of 14,548.9 kg |

Both are correct and they answer different questions — capacity lost versus material lost. **The plant reports the kilogram basis (3.09%), and both tabs agree on it.** Show both with the basis named (**R-22**). Do not present one as the rejection figure without saying which it is.

**Note a correction:** summing the two kilogram columns is **legitimate** — `DAILY REPORT` does exactly that (446.40 ≈ 449.3). The original defect was labelling that kilogram sum as litres, not the sum itself. The rulebook's R-09 wording should be corrected accordingly.

**Warn on a degenerate join** (**R-27**) — if the union produces no matched dates while both sources hold rows, that is a failure, not an empty month.
