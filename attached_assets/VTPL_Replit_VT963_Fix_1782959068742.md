# VT963 — Sanity-check findings (observed issues + one question for the engine)

VT963 (1,576 MT, 38 angle sections) was run through the C1–C14 check. It surfaced issues that the smaller VT948 did not. Below are the **observed** problems (facts from the output files) and the fixes. One issue has an **unknown cause** — a question for you to answer, not an assumption on our part. Standard R6 lengths only (fine-bar/off-band out of scope).

Job totals: Plan A 4.14%, Plan B 3.97% (agency 3.45%). C1, C4, C5, C6, C9, C10, C11, C12, C13, C14 all PASS. The failures below are C7, C3, C2, and C8.

---

## Issue 1 (top priority) — C7: off-grid bar lengths, and a K-cap violation. **Cause unknown — please identify.**

**Observed (both plans):**
- **75X75X5 MS** (B1, 11.5 MT, hard K cap = 2) ships at **K = 8**, and three of its bar lengths are **off the R6 grid: 6800, 7000, 7100 mm**. Each of those off-grid bars holds a **single long piece** (6573 / 6822 / 6828 mm). The section is RED at 5.14%.
- **80X80X6 HT** (B1) uses an off-grid **6900 mm** bar, holding a single long piece (6723 / 6719 mm).

The R6 B1 grid is 8.0–13.0 m in 500 mm steps, so 6800/7000/7100/6900 mm are all off-grid, and none is a permitted standard length.

**We are NOT assuming what produced these.** We do not know whether the engine has an off-standard/exception length path implemented, and if so whether it fired here. So the question first:

> **Question for the engine:** What produced the off-grid lengths (6800/7000/7100 mm on 75X75X5 MS, 6900 mm on 80X80X6 HT)? Is there an implemented code path that can emit non-standard bar lengths (an exception, a "fit-to-piece" fallback, or otherwise)? If so, what triggered it on these sections, and why did 75X75X5 MS reach K = 8 when its hard cap is 2?

**Required fix regardless of the cause:** re-solve both sections on **standard R6 lengths only**, within the hard K cap (75X75X5 MS ≤ 2, 80X80X6 HT ≤ its tier cap). The long pieces (≈6.5–6.8 m) should be packed onto standard bars together with the section's short pieces as fillers — e.g. 75X75X5 MS has many short marks (1269–1478 mm and 2.0–3.7 m) that can fill the tail of a standard bar carrying a long piece, rather than each long piece taking its own custom bar. No off-grid lengths in the output.

If the off-grid path is an implemented feature: given fine-bar/off-band is parked, it should be **disabled** for these plans so no non-standard length is ever emitted. Confirm whether it exists and that it is now off.

## Issue 2 — C3: wasteful singleton bars

Bars carrying a single piece with very large end-scrap (leftover-on-a-full-bar):

| Plan | Section | Bar | Scrap |
|---|---|---|---|
| A and B | **55X55X5 MS** | 12000 mm, 1 piece | 9,574 mm |
| A | **65X65X5 MS** | 11000 mm, 1 piece | 9,314 mm |
| A | **90X90X6 HT** | 9500 mm, 1 piece | 2,738 mm |

**Fix:** apply the stranded-piece fix — move each lone piece into the unused tail of another bar in the same section (respecting ≤3 marks/bar and the R3 ladder), delete the emptied bar, add no new length. Apply per plan wherever the section appears (55X55X5 MS is in both). If no valid host exists on the section's existing R6 lengths, leave it and mark it geometry-locked.

## Issue 3 — C2: Plan B section under its K cap with high waste

- **90X90X6 HT** (Plan B): 21.3 MT, so hard cap = 3, but ships at **K = 2** on [8000, 9500] and wastes **9.4%**.

**Fix:** allow this section to use up to K = 3 on the R6 grid and adopt the lower-steel result if it saves > 1 pp (the same K-is-a-ceiling rule already applied elsewhere). Standard lengths only.

(Note: 90X90X6 HT also appears in Issues 1 and 2 signals — treat it as one section needing a clean re-solve within cap 3 on standard lengths.)

## Issue 4 — C8: reconciliation flag is corrupt indent data, NOT a plan bug

C8 shows a net mismatch (checker read 1,140,675 kg from the indent vs the plans' 1,107,232 kg). As on VT948, this is driven by corrupt rows in the **indent file** (inflated piece quantities on some sections), not by the plans — the two plans agree with each other on net. No plan fix needed; the indent source data should be cleaned. Do not "fix" the plans for this.
