# PROMPT — Expose `mp_scheduler` for Plumbing capacity fitting

**Repository:** `fonnjain/Prayag-Plant-MC-Analysis` · `prayag-plant.com`

Your Q1–Q7 answers settled the design. **Building this.**

---

## What was decided from your answers

**Plumbing Pass 2 will call this app.** `mp_scheduler` is live, operator-facing, and resolves the shared-machine problem — MC3, MC4, MC5 across CPVC, UPVC, SWR and AGRI — by construction, because it assigns real machine-hours rather than summing envelopes that were never additive.

**PTMT will not.** Your answer that PTMT has no routing, no BOM weights and no rates was decisive. PTMT gets a simpler category-p90 fitter in the planning app. **Do not build PTMT support here.**

**And your recommendation is accepted:** a **preview-only, non-persistent** endpoint in the Flask app, with the planning app sending normalised engine-compatible demand rather than a bare payload. That is your **1–2 engineer-day** estimate rather than the 3–5 day one, and it keeps demand ownership where it belongs.

# What to build

```
POST /data-api/v1/schedule
X-API-Key: <key>
```

## Request

The planning app already produces this — `GET /api/plan/runs/:id/schedule-request` exists and returns item code, colour, quantity, material, weight, category and urgency rank per line.

**Tell us the exact shape you want** and the planning app will conform. Specifically:

- which fields the engine needs to resolve BOM, routing, rates and capable machines
- whether `material` should be `CPVC` / `UPVC` / `SWR` / `AGRI` or your internal codes
- whether `weight` is useful or the engine resolves it from `mp_bom_weight` regardless
- how you want fitting demand distinguished from pipe demand

**No W1–W4 will be sent.** Per your Q4 answer, absent weeks means `first_requested_week = 1` and the existing priority rule derives the placement — W1-origin fills W1 capacity, remainder cascades into W2 ahead of W2's own demand. **That is exactly the behaviour we want**, and the reason we are not pre-splitting the month.

## Response

`ScheduleResult.to_dict()` already carries everything needed:

```
blocks                  day/shift assignments
weekly_fill             what fits each week
unfinished              the cannot-be-made list
total_capacity_hrs
total_scheduled_hrs
total_idle_hrs
week_days
downtime_machine_days
downtime_hours_lost
capacity_advisory
```

**No renaming required** — the planning app will map. Return the native shape.

## ⚠ One conversion the planning app cannot do

**`unfinished` comes back in kg. The planning app works in pieces.**

It has no BOM and cannot convert back. **Please return unfinished quantity in pieces as well as kg**, or include the per-item conversion factor used. Otherwise the cannot-be-made figure reaches the floor in the wrong unit.

## Non-persistent

No run identity, no storage, no freeze semantics. **The planning app owns demand and persists the resulting plan.** Per your recommendation, a shared ownership model is a separate deliberate design, not something to acquire by accident.

# ⚠ The Sunday problem — fix this before or alongside

Your Q6 answer confirmed the risk, and it is the same defect class that once discarded **80,522 pieces** from PTMT monitoring.

The scheduler builds synthetic day slots from `week_days = [6,6,6,7]` and **cannot tell which slot is a Sunday or whether one was worked.** Meanwhile the planning app's calendar counts non-Sundays **plus any Sunday with production** — August had three worked Sundays, giving 29 working days rather than 26.

**So a worked Sunday is counted by planning and excluded from scheduler capacity**, unless someone manually configures a larger total.

**Two options, and this needs deciding rather than deferring:**

**Accept `week_days` from the request.** The planning app computes the Sunday-aware split and sends `[6,7,6,10]` or whatever the month gives. Simplest, and the calling app already has the calendar.

**Or make the scheduler date-aware** — real dates, worked-Sunday recognition. Better long-term, considerably more work, and out of scope here.

**Recommend the first**, with `week_days` as a required request field so it can never silently fall back to `[6,6,6,7]`.

# Verification

Once built, run one Plumbing month end to end and report:

```
demand sent            pieces, item count
scheduled              pieces and kg
unfinished             pieces and kg
capacity hrs           scheduled / idle / total
week_days used         confirm it matches what was sent
```

**Two specific checks:**

**Shared machines are not over-committed.** MC3, MC4 and MC5 appear in multiple material envelopes. Assert no machine is assigned beyond its available hours in any day or shift. **This is the single largest gain over a category-level fitter and should be proven, not assumed.**

**And the two locked-out machines are excluded** from available capacity, with their absence reported rather than silently reducing the total.

# Do not

- **Build PTMT support.** Routing, BOM and rates do not exist for it; PTMT is being handled in the planning app.
- **Change any metric calculation.** Rejection, utilisation and output efficiency are settled and validated against Prayag's own figures. **They must not move.**
- **Add persistence or freeze semantics.** Preview only.
